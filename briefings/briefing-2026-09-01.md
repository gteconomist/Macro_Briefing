## Top of mind

The rate picture coming into September is the dominant macro backdrop: the 10Y closed last week at 4.73% (+6 bps on the day, +14 bps on the week after the Jackson Hole-adjacent volatility), the 2Y jumped 14 bps in a single session to 4.34%, and the 10Y–3M spread has pushed to +84 bps — the curve's most positive read in some time. With the Atlanta Fed GDPNow tracking Q3 at 4.61% (up from 1.54% just a quarter prior), the question is no longer whether the economy is soft-landing but whether the Fed's 3.50–3.75% target range is anywhere near restrictive enough. Today's ISM Manufacturing and JOLTS prints at 10:00 are the first hard data of the month and will set the tone heading into Friday's employment report.

## Fed watch

Target range is 3.50–3.75%, effective fed funds at 3.63%, SOFR at 3.65% — both sitting comfortably inside the corridor with no anomalies. The 14-bp one-day move in the 2Y on 8/28 and the 10Y at 4.73% reflect markets ratcheting back rate-cut expectations materially; the term premium component of the 30Y at 5.22% is now doing real work. No FOMC meeting, minutes, Beige Book, or SEP release appears in the week-ahead calendar, so the Fed's next formal communication opportunity is beyond this week — Friday's employment report will effectively be the loudest voice in the room on the policy path.
