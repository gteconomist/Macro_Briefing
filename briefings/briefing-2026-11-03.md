## Today's calendar (Tue 11/3, ET)

- **Factory Orders (Full Report), 10:00** — September durable goods were already released; today adds non-durables and the complete shipments/inventories detail. With the I/S ratios (inventory-to-shipments) for manufacturing at 152.2 (up from 150.5 in August) and I/Q ratios at 170.8, watch whether order backlogs are building or inventories are accumulating passively — that distinction has direct implications for Q4 industrial output.
- **JOLTS (August), 10:00** — FRED shows the latest available JOLTS observation as of August at 7,079K openings, down from 7,335K in July; today's release should update to September. The trend matters more than the level at this point — a continued drift toward 7.0M or below would mark a meaningful cooling from the 12M+ peak and further support the Fed's assessment that labor demand is normalizing.
