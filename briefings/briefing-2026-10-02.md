## Today's calendar (Fri 10/2, ET)

- **Employment Report, 8:30** — September NFP is the number. The August read was +162K with unemployment at 4.1% and average hourly earnings at $32.53 (+$0.11 m/m). Watch whether payroll growth accelerates or stalls, whether the unemployment rate holds at 4.1%, and whether wages push above the $32.53 level in a way that complicates the Fed's easing path. - **Mon 10/5:** ISM Services — after Manufacturing's apparent softening in regional surveys, the services print will determine whether the broader economy is decelerating or just goods-side. Services has been the primary driver of core PCE stickiness.

## Fed watch

The target range remains 3.75%–4.00% (DFEDTARL/DFEDTARU), with the effective fed funds rate at 3.88% (as of 9/30) and SOFR at 3.90% (as of 9/30) — both sitting near the midpoint and top of the corridor, respectively, consistent with normal reserve conditions. The 2-year closed Thursday at 4.78% (down 10 bps on the day), leaving the 2s10s spread at +46 bps and the 10y-3m spread fully uninverted at +107 bps — the curve's continued steepening reflects a market that has priced meaningful cuts but is now demanding a larger term premium as fiscal concerns and energy-driven inflation risk linger. No FOMC meeting, minutes, Beige Book, or SEP release appears in the week-ahead calendar; the September employment report today is the dominant input into how the Committee frames its next move.
