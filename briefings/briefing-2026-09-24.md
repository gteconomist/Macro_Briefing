## Top of mind

The dominant story is a sharp, broad-based selloff in Treasuries Wednesday: the 10-year yield jumped 15 bps to 5.11%, the 2-year surged 14 bps to 4.85%, and the 30-year hit 5.40% (+11 bps). The 10s/3m spread widened to +92 bps from +80 bps, confirming the curve is continuing to bear-steepen. Brent front-month (BZ) settled up sharply at $103.08 — a $3.83 move — while WTI (CL) dropped $2.43 to $92.16, an unusual divergence that warrants watching for physical vs. paper dislocation. The rate move is the macro story of the morning; at 5.11%, the 10-year is at a level that meaningfully tightens financial conditions independent of anything the Fed does.
