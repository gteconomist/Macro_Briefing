## Fed watch

The target range remains 3.50–3.75%, with the effective fed funds rate at 3.63% and SOFR at 3.64% — both hugging the midpoint cleanly. The FOMC statement drops Wednesday at 2:00 PM; no SEP or press conference is scheduled this meeting cycle per the calendar, so the statement itself carries full signaling weight.
