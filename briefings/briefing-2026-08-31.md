## Top of mind

The dominant macro story entering the week is the geopolitical risk premium now embedded in commodity and risk markets. U.S. strikes on Iran's Larak Island and Iranian retaliatory attacks on Jordan and the UAE over the weekend inject meaningful uncertainty into energy supply chains — yet WTI closed the prior session at $83.90 (down from $86.34) and Brent at $88.24 (down from $92.71), moves that predate the weekend's escalation. Monday's open will be the first pricing of this conflict news. Treasury Secretary Bessent heading to the G20 to rally allies on Iran while tariff tensions strain those same alliances adds a second-order complication for trade policy and allied coordination.
