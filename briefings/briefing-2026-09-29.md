## Top of mind

The yield curve is the dominant story: T10Y3M closed Monday at +96 bps (up from +93 bps Friday), the steepest read in the current data window, with the 10-year at 5.17% and the 30-year at 5.49% as of 9/25. ## Fed watch

Target range is 3.75–4.00% (DFEDTARU 4.00, DFEDTARL 3.75, both confirmed as of 9/28). The effective fed funds rate (DFF) is 3.88% as of 9/25; SOFR printed 3.90% on the same date, essentially flat to the prior day and well within the corridor. The curve's continued steepening — 30-year at 549 bps, the 10s/3m spread pushing to +96 bps — is the market's way of saying it believes the Fed will hold short rates roughly here but is demanding a significant term premium for anything beyond the near horizon. No FOMC meeting, minutes, Beige Book, or SEP release appears in the week-ahead calendar, so the PCE print Wednesday and payrolls Friday are the primary inputs into how the next meeting's calculus shifts.
