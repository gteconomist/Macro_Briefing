## Top of mind

Friday's September jobs report landed with nonfarm payrolls at 159,044K — a gain of just 29K over August — and unemployment ticking up to 4.2% from 4.1%, while average hourly earnings rose to $32.60 (+$0.07 m/m). That's a materially soft print against a backdrop where the 10-year is at 5.28% (as of 10/2) and the 2-year at 4.83%, leaving the term premium doing heavy lifting even as the T10Y3M spread holds at +109 bps. The combination of labor market deceleration and still-elevated long rates is the central tension heading into this week's data.

## Fed watch

Target range holds at 3.75–4.00%, with the effective fed funds rate at 3.88% (as of 10/2) and SOFR at 3.88% — both sitting right at the midpoint of the band. The long end continues to run well above the policy rate: the 10-year at 5.28% and the 30-year at 5.63% (both as of 10/2) reflect either term premium re-pricing, fiscal concerns, or both — none of which the FOMC directly controls. Wednesday's FOMC Minutes are the week's key Fed event; given the labor market softening in September, the minutes will be read closely for how much weight the Committee placed on downside growth risks versus residual inflation persistence at the time of the meeting.
