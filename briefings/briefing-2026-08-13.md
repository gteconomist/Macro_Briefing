## Top of mind

July CPI dropped yesterday and is the dominant data story this week. CPIAUCSL moved from 332.568 to 332.813 — a 0.245-point monthly gain — while core (CPILFESL) rose 0.724 points to 336.789, the largest of the two moves and the one the Fed is watching most closely given still-elevated services inflation. Set against a 10-year at 4.70% and a 30-year at 5.24%, the real rate environment remains meaningfully restrictive even after 100+ bps of cuts from the cycle peak. This morning's PPI will either confirm or complicate the pipeline inflation picture before Friday's consumer sentiment read closes the week.
