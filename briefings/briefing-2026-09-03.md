## This week ahead

- **Fri 9/4:** **Employment Report (BLS, 8:30)** — The week's marquee release. ## Fed watch

Target range is 3.50–3.75%, with the effective fed funds rate at 3.63% (as of 9/1) and SOFR at 3.66% — both sitting comfortably inside the corridor. The 10Y–3M spread has turned positive at +87 bps (flat for two days), a notable shift from the extended inversion that characterized most of 2024–25. No FOMC meeting, minutes, Beige Book, or SEP release appears in the calendar for the next five business days. The immediate Fed story is how tomorrow's Employment Report and the oil-driven inflation signal interact with the current dot plot — a second consecutive weak payroll print plus energy-led CPI pressure would put the September meeting framing in play well before that decision date.
