## Fed watch

The target range remains 3.75–4.00% (DFEDTARU/DFEDTARL as of 11/4), with the effective fed funds rate at 3.88% (as of 11/3) and SOFR at 3.97% (as of 11/3) — the SOFR/EFFR spread of roughly 9 bps is within normal. The curve has steepened meaningfully: the 10Y–3M spread is +69 bps (up from +74 bps the day prior but still solidly positive), and the 10Y–2Y is +32 bps, both consistent with markets pricing a structurally higher terminal rate than the current policy setting. No FOMC meeting, minutes, Beige Book, or SEP release appears in the calendar for the remainder of this week; the December meeting will be the next live event, and Friday's employment report will be the single most important data input between now and that decision.
