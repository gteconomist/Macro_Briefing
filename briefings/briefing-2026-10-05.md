## Top of mind

Friday's September employment report is the week's defining data point. Nonfarm payrolls came in at 159,044K — net gain of just 29K over August — with unemployment ticking up to 4.2% and average hourly earnings at $32.60 (+$0.07 MoM). The labor market is clearly downshifting, and markets absorbed it with a modest risk-on close (S&P 500 +0.73% to 7,722; Nasdaq +1.19% to 27,191). The more consequential transmission mechanism: the 10-year Treasury settled at 5.28% Friday — up 4 bps on the day, 30-year at 5.63% — suggesting the bond market is still pricing term premium and fiscal risk alongside softer labor data, not rallying on it. That divergence between equity relief and rates stubbornness is the core tension entering the week.
